# Automatic differentiation discovery

Reviewed on 2026-11-12. This discovery view groups existing catalog records; it adds no GAMS classifications or execution-verification claims. The curated topic is independent of inconsistent author-supplied GitHub topics.

Use [the automatic differentiation page](https://beliavsky.github.io/fortran-gams-catalog/topics/automatic-differentiation/) or the Differentiation selector. Share filters with `?topic=automatic-differentiation`, `forward-mode`, `reverse-mode`, `higher-derivatives`, `sparse-jacobians`, `matrix-derivatives`, or `source-transformation`. They combine with source-review, license, collection and FPM listing filters. The selection follows internal navigation; topic-specific GAMS counts respect license, source-review and FPM exclusions.

Automatic differentiation propagates derivatives through arithmetic or computation graphs. It does not by itself establish symbolic expression manipulation. Numerical derivative implementations retain H1 mappings; helpers can remain under software-development classifications. O has a related-topic link rather than receiving blanket AD mappings.

Facets summarize recorded capabilities. ?Only source-reviewed mappings? filters GAMS evidence, not each method facet or derivative order. In particular, some higher-order or forward-mode capabilities are documented beyond the narrower source review. No programs were newly compiled or run.
